# 💳 ExpensifyPro: Premium Financial Suite

ExpensifyPro is a high-performance, real-time financial management application designed for freelancers, social media marketers, and power users. It combines traditional expense tracking with a specialized **Partner Income Module** and advanced **Historical Reporting**.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. Unified Command Dashboard
*   **Real-Time Stats**: Instant visibility into your **Total Balance**, **Cash In Hand**, and **Account Balance**.
*   **Dynamic Visuals**: Spending overview chart and "Major Categories" breakdown to see where your money goes.
*   **Monthly Snapshot**: Automatically tracks your spending habits for the current month.

### 2. Specialized Income Tracking (Partner Income)
Designed specifically for marketers and collaborators who share project earnings:
*   **Real-time Calculation**: Input a total project value and your specific share percentage; the app calculates your profit instantly.
*   **Payment Status**: Track payments as `Pending` (Partner received it) or `Received` (You received your share).
*   **Method Control**: Choose whether earnings go to your **Cash** or **Bank Account**.
*   **Project History**: Full log of all shared projects with search and filter capabilities.

### 3. Advanced Transactions Engine
*   **High-Speed Logging**: Add transactions with categories, descriptions, and payment methods in seconds.
*   **DataTables Integration**: Search, sort, and filter through thousands of entries with zero lag.
*   **Full CRUD**: Edit or delete any entry with immediate balance synchronization.

### 4. Financial Intelligence Reports
*   **Dual-Filter System**: Select a specific **Year** and **Month** to travel back in time.
*   **Historical Balance Power Cards**: See your exact bank and cash balances as they were at the **end of any selected month**.
*   **Profit/Loss Analysis**:
    *   **Total Income**: Pure earned revenue.
    *   **Total Expense**: Raw spending (Cash Out).
    *   **Cash In (Returns)**: Total of refunds and returns.
    *   **Net Cash Flow**: Actual spending movement (Out - In).
    *   **Net Balance**: Period-specific performance (Profit/Loss).
*   **Interactive Charts**: Doughnut charts for category spending and Bar charts for Income vs. Expenses.

### 5. Customization & Infrastructure
*   **Category Manager**: Create custom categories with hundreds of FontAwesome icons and sleek Tailwind colors.
*   **Opening Balances**: Set your initial Cash and Bank balances in Settings for 100% mathematical accuracy.
*   **Dark Mode**: A beautiful, premium dark aesthetic that saves battery and looks professional.
*   **Google Sheets Backend**: Your data is yours. Hosted securely on Google Sheets with automated syncing.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
*   **Frontend**: HTML5, Vanilla CSS3 (Tailwind CSS), JavaScript (ES6+).
*   **Database**: Google Sheets API via Google Apps Script.
*   **Charts**: Chart.js for beautiful, responsive data visualization.
*   **UI Components**: SweetAlert2, DataTables, FontAwesome 6.

## 📱 Mobile Experience
ExpensifyPro is fully responsive with a **Bottom Navigation Bar**, making it feel like a native mobile app on your phone.
